Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23rd, 2026

Dash Fusion ("we", "our", or "us") operates the List Calculator app (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use the App.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from users. The App is designed to help you calculate any list that you create. Your lists and their contents are stored locally on your device. You can use the App without providing any personal information or creating an account.

2. Ads

The App uses third-party services that may collect certain information to display advertisements. These services may use cookies or other technologies to serve ads based on your interactions with the App. The ads displayed are provided by Google AdMob.

For more information on how AdMob uses data, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms.

Users who subscribe to List Calculator Premium will no longer see ads in the App.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

The App offers an optional paid subscription ("List Calculator Premium") that removes advertisements. All purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, receive, or store your payment details (such as card numbers). Google Play handles the transaction and only provides us with confirmation of your subscription status so the App can enable or disable premium features.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Currency Exchange Rates

If you use lists with more than one currency, the App fetches publicly available currency exchange rates from frankfurter.app (a free, European Central Bank-sourced exchange rate service) in order to convert and total amounts across different currencies. These requests contain only a base currency code (for example, "USD"). No personal data, list contents, or device identifiers are sent in these requests. Exchange rates are cached on your device to reduce network usage.

For more information, please review the frankfurter.app website.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data to provide their functionality:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying banner and interstitial ads (shown only to non-subscribers).
  • Google Play Billing — for processing optional Premium subscriptions.
  • frankfurter.app — for fetching public currency exchange rates (only when multiple currencies are in use).

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services to understand how they handle your data.

6. Data Security

While no personal information is collected by the App, we are committed to maintaining a safe and secure experience for all users. However, please note that no data transmission over the internet or electronic storage method is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at panji.tito@outlook.com.
